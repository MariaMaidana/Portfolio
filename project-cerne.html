Cerne.

Identify tree species, measure trees with augmented reality, and explore estimated CO₂ capture through a collaborative map.

About the project

From measurements to a collaborative map

↗ App Store

Cerne identifies tree species from photos and uses augmented reality to measure a tree's trunk and height. With this information, it estimates carbon capture.

Each entry contributes to a collaborative biodiversity map, bringing information about trees and green spaces together.
