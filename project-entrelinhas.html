Otterlines

An interactive reading experience for families, made for Apple TV.

About the project

Reading together on the big screen

↗ App Store

Otterlines brings stories and activities to the TV, with the otters Sagu and Pudim guiding children through each adventure.

The experience invites families to read together and explore questions and creative activities along the way. It was built as a team project for Apple TV.
