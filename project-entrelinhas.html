Otterlines

Books, optional narration, and activities help children and adults build a reading habit together on Apple TV and iPad.

About the project

Reading together, on and off screen

↗ App Store

Designed for children ages 6 to 8, Otterlines brings children and adults together around reading. Guided by Sagu and Pudim, children explore a story map with optional narration and literary activities.

Activities include story questions, sentence completion, spelling, and drawing. The Reading Corner pairs physical books with sounds and a timer, carrying the experience beyond the screen.

I’m one of the developers on Otterlines, built with the Apple Developer Academy team and presented at Tecnopuc Experience 2026. The project aims to support reading comprehension, creativity, and vocabulary.
