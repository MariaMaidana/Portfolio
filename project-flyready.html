FlyReady

Practice cabin crew interviews out loud, with questions that respond to what you say.

About the project

Interview practice that talks back

↗ App Store

FlyReady runs a spoken interview on your iPhone. A recruiter asks a question, listens to your answer, and follows up based on your response.

Sessions cover motivation, customer conflict, teamwork, safety, adaptability, and behavioral questions. After each answer, you get feedback you can use for the next round. Speech and feedback run on the device.
